Privacy Policy

How we handle and protect your data at NiDi.

A. Information Collection and Use

We collect personal information such as your name, email address, and chat interactions via Google Authentication to provide and improve our services. This information is used to personalize your experience and optimize the chatbot's performance.

B. Data Protection and Storage

Your details are stored securely using industry-standard measures to prevent unauthorized access. We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.

C. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or trade your personal data. We may share information only when legally required or to protect our rights. Third-party service providers assisting us are obligated to protect your information and use it solely for the requested service.

D. Cookies and Sessions

We use secure session cookies to keep you logged in and enhance your experience. Disabling cookies may affect your ability to access the chat and locator features.

E. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal information. Since we use Google Auth, you can also manage app permissions through your Google Account settings.

By using NIDI LawBot, you agree to this Privacy Policy. Updates will be posted on this page periodically.
